## 💡 Our Solution: AI Expert-Powered Education

Miss Information combines real-time content analysis with AI expert consultation to create an engaging, educational experience that teaches critical thinking through hands-on practice.

## 🎮 How It Works

### 1. Real-Time Content Analysis
- Live RSS feed scanning from credible news sources
- Wikipedia cross-reference verification
- Advanced claim extraction and categorization
- Source credibility scoring

### 2. Interactive Fact-Checking Game
- **Detection Mode**: Identify altered misinformation in articles
- **Verification Mode**: Find unverified claims using Wikipedia
- Dynamic scoring with educational feedback
- Category-based challenges (Climate, Energy, Tech, etc.)

### 3. AI Expert Consultation (Tavus Integration)
- **Post-Game Analysis**: Personalized video explanations after each round
- **Real-time Help**: "Ask Expert" button available during gameplay
- **Category Matching**: Appropriate expert automatically selected
- **Custom Scripts**: AI-generated explanations based on specific game results
